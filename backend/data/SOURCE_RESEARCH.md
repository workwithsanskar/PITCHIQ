# External source evaluation for PITCHIQ

- FBref coverage: https://fbref.com/en/about/coverage
  Broad player-season and competition coverage. Review current site terms, robots/access policy, and data licensing before automated collection or redistribution.
- StatsBomb Open Data: https://github.com/statsbomb/open-data
  Selected open competitions with matches, lineups and event files. Attribute StatsBomb in published work based on the data.
- football-data.org: https://www.football-data.org/documentation/api
  Structured competition/team/squad resources. The published free plan is limited; squad data is listed under paid plans: https://www.football-data.org/pricing

Data sourcing policy:
Do not scrape or redistribute a provider's database unless its terms permit it. Prefer documented APIs, open datasets, and explicit attribution. Track source and retrieval date for each imported record.
